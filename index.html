Keep your child on Pip

On iPhone or iPad, Guided Access locks the screen to Pip so the address bar, tabs and other apps can't be tapped:

  1. Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access → turn it on and set a passcode.
  2. Open Pip in Safari, then press the side (or top, or Home) button three times quickly.
  3. Draw a circle around Safari's address bar and tabs to block them, then tap Start.
  4. To finish, press the button three times again and type the passcode.
